Buyer desk

One intake point for the sales team

The form captures product interest, budget, size, style preference, urgency, source, and notes. The local desk forwards the submission to the published n8n webhook.

Workflow path

Buyer form to follow-up

  1. Buyer submits product interest and preferences.
  2. n8n normalizes fields and segments buyer intent.
  3. Google Sheets receives the structured buyer record.
  4. Gmail sends buyer confirmation and sales alert messages.
  5. Ready-to-buy shoppers enter the personal shopping follow-up path.
